Founded by Y Combinator alum and former machine learning engineer Karan Grover, Seattle-based Kanu AI has raised $11.7 million to solve a persistent enterprise problem: valuable operational knowledge getting trapped in employees' heads and spreadsheets. Led by Trilogy Equity Partners, the new funding will help scale Kanu's platform, which automatically converts day-to-day employee workflows into permanent, production-grade software deployed directly within a company's cloud.
