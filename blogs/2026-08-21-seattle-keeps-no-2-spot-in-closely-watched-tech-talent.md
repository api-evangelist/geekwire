The region held onto the No. 2 spot in CBRE's annual tech talent scorecard, behind only the Bay Area. The report also flagged Seattle's office vacancy rate, the highest of the 50 markets ranked, and a population of 20-somethings that shrank between 2019 and 2024, raising questions about the pipeline.
