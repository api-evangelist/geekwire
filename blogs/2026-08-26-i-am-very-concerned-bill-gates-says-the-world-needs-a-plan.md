Bill Gates says the "turbulent AI era" has arrived, and the AI industry is crossing safety lines it once promised to respect. In a new essay and an interview with GeekWire, he lays out three ideas for managing what's coming: new national and global institutions, a category of jobs reserved for humans, and a tax on AI and robots.
