Kalshi is still operating in Washington nearly three weeks after a Seattle judge granted the state a preliminary injunction against the prediction market platform. The New York-based company has since appealed to the Washington Court of Appeals and brought in former U.S. Acting Solicitor General Neal Katyal for its defense in a case with national implications.
